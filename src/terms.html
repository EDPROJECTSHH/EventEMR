Clinical responsibility stays with the clinician. This application records care and offers reference material and dose calculations as an aid. It does not diagnose, prescribe or decide. Every dose, calculation and clinical decision must be verified by the treating clinician against their own judgement and their organisation's protocols before it is acted on. It is not a certified medical device and must not be relied on as the sole basis for treatment.

Who may use it

  • Access is for staff authorised by the organisation running the event — clinical staff, ambulance crews and the event's command post.
  • Each device is bound to one post for the duration of an event. Entering records for a post you are not working at, or under another person's name, misattributes clinical care and is not permitted.
  • Configuration passcodes are issued for operational control, not as a security boundary. Do not share them outside the team, and do not use the application on a device you do not control.

Patient information

  • Record only what is clinically necessary, and record it accurately.
  • Patient information in this application is confidential. Do not export, screenshot or forward records outside the channels your organisation has approved.
  • Borrowed or shared devices must be returned to the organisation at the end of an event so that the working copy can be closed and cleared.
  • How information is handled is described in the Privacy Notice.

Availability

The application is built to keep working without a network connection, holding records on the device and merging them when a connection returns. Even so, no guarantee is made that it will be available, reachable or synchronised at any particular moment. Event medical teams must keep a workable fallback — paper records — and must not let the absence of the application delay patient care.
